# Frequently Asked Questions

## About MVPCompanion

### What is MVPCompanion?
MVPCompanion guides you step by step from an initial app idea to a clear, structured, and buildable MVP specification. It helps you clarify your idea, identify the right users, define your product's value, prioritize features, and prepare for development.

### What does MVP stand for?
MVP stands for Minimum Viable Product. It is the smallest coherent version of a product that delivers meaningful value to its intended users and helps you test your most important assumptions.

### Who is MVPCompanion for?
MVPCompanion is designed for aspiring founders, entrepreneurs, developers, product managers, and anyone who wants to turn an app idea into a well-defined product. It is particularly useful if you have a promising idea but are unsure how to structure it before development begins.

### Do I need technical knowledge to use MVPCompanion?
No. You can start with a simple idea and a basic understanding of the problem you want to solve. MVPCompanion helps you clarify the product first and address technical decisions when they become relevant.

## The MVP Development Process

### How does MVPCompanion work?
MVPCompanion guides you through a structured process that progressively transforms your idea into an MVP specification. You clarify the problem and target users, establish your value proposition, define the product and its core features, design the user experience, and specify the requirements for development.

### Why shouldn't I start by building features?
Starting with features can lead to unnecessary complexity and wasted development effort. MVPCompanion helps you understand the problem, the user, and the desired outcome first. You then derive the features needed to deliver that value.

### What if I don't know all the answers at the beginning?
That's perfectly normal. The process helps you identify assumptions, clarify uncertainties, and determine what needs further investigation. You can refine your decisions as your understanding improves.

### Can I change my idea while working through the process?
Absolutely. New insights may reveal a better target audience, a more important problem, or a different solution. The goal is not to preserve your original idea at all costs, but to develop the strongest product concept based on what you learn.

## Defining Your MVP

### How do I decide which features belong in my MVP?
Every feature should support an important user need or help test a critical product assumption. MVPCompanion helps you distinguish essential features from improvements that can wait until later and functionality that should be excluded from the initial version.

### How small should my MVP be?
Your MVP should be as small as possible while still delivering the core value of your product. It must provide a coherent user experience rather than a collection of incomplete features.

### Is an MVP the same as a prototype?
No. A prototype helps explore or demonstrate an idea, design, or interaction. An MVP is intended to deliver real value to its intended users while helping you learn whether your product assumptions hold up in practice. A prototype can be a useful step toward an MVP.

### Should I include AI in my MVP?
Only if AI helps solve a specific user problem and contributes meaningful value. The technology should serve the product's purpose, not drive the feature list. MVPCompanion encourages you to define the role of AI and its requirements before committing to implementation.

## From Specification to Development

### What will I have when I complete the process?
You will have a structured MVP specification describing your product concept, target users, value proposition, core use cases, prioritized features, user flows, data model, functional requirements, acceptance criteria, technical boundaries, and open questions.

### Why do I need an MVP specification before development?
A clear specification gives you and your development team a shared understanding of what needs to be built and why. It reduces ambiguity, supports more realistic effort estimates, and helps prevent costly misunderstandings during implementation.

### Can I use the specification with an AI coding tool or a developer?
Yes. The specification is intended to provide a structured foundation for implementation. You can use it to brief a developer, work with a development agency, or guide an AI coding tool. The specification may still need to be adapted to the chosen technology and implementation approach.

### Does completing the process guarantee that my product will succeed?
No. A structured process reduces avoidable risks but cannot guarantee market demand or commercial success. The purpose of the MVP is to test important assumptions, learn from real users, and make better decisions about what to do next.

### What should I do after completing my MVP specification?
Review the remaining assumptions and technical risks, estimate the development effort, and prepare to build your MVP. Once it is ready, test it with intended users, measure the results, and use what you learn to decide how the product should evolve.

## The MVPCompanion Philosophy

### What makes MVPCompanion different from simply using an AI app builder?
An AI app builder helps you create software. MVPCompanion focuses on the product decisions that should come first: which problem to solve, for whom, what value to deliver, and what is actually necessary to build. A clear specification can then help you use an AI app builder more effectively.

### What is the most important principle behind MVPCompanion?
Clarify before designing, define the product before listing features, establish the MVP scope before specifying implementation, and decide how you will measure success before building. The objective is not simply to build less software, but to build the right product first.
